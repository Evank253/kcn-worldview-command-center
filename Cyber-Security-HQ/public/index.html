DeFlock — Privacy Features
DeFlock (deflock.me / deflock.org) is a privacy-first, community-driven project that maps public ALPR and surveillance infrastructure.
  • No personal data collection — no cookies, analytics, or tracking. Local storage only for theme & first-visit message.
  • OpenStreetMap based — camera locations are contributed to the public OSM commons.
  • Offline capable mobile app with manufacturer profiles (Flock, Motorola, Genetec, etc.).
  • Route avoidance — can generate driving routes that minimize or avoid known ALPR cameras (adjustable buffer).
  • Proximity alerts when approaching mapped devices.
  • Free, open-source, no ads.
Primary map: deflock.me
Other Surveillance Mapping Tools
  • Atlas of Surveillance (EFF + University of Nevada) — searchable database of police tech including ALPRs, drones, facial recognition, body cameras. Highly regarded for research quality.
  • Eyes on Flock / Have I Been Flocked — transparency portals and FOIA-derived audit log tools.
  • OpenStreetMap itself (query surveillance:type=ALPR) powers many of these maps.
Legal Access to Flock Footage — Clarified
  • No public live streams — Flock feeds are not openly viewable by the public.
  • Law enforcement / authorized customers access data through Flock's platform under agency contracts.
  • Public records / FOIA — In some jurisdictions (notably certain Washington state court rulings), Flock images have been ruled public records subject to disclosure requests. Outcomes vary by state; retention is often ~30 days, so timely requests matter. Always follow official public-records procedures.
  • Unauthorized access is illegal. This platform provides awareness only.
This section is for transparency, research, and defensive awareness. It does not provide or encourage unauthorized access to any system.
