# Texture attribution

## Day, night, and specular maps
`earth_day_4096.jpg`, `earth_night_4096.jpg`, `earth_specular_4096.jpg`

Resized (8192x4096 -> 4096x2048) from the static source images in
[matteason/daily-cloud-maps](https://github.com/matteason/daily-cloud-maps),
released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain) by Matt Eason. Original
data is NASA imagery (public domain, US government work).
