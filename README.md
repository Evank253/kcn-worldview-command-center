## Systems Overview

### 1. KCN Worldview
Intelligence / knowledge-graph platform. Interactive Three.js globe, entity relationships, multi-source fusion layers, AI reasoning panel.

### 2. KCN Cyber Security Headquarters
- Dedicated cyber-focused 3D globe
- Wireshark-style live event list
- Systems health monitoring
- **OSINT & Surveillance tab** covering:
  - DeFlock privacy features & camera mapping
  - Atlas of Surveillance (EFF)
  - FOIA / public-records request strategies for ALPR data
  - Legal access clarifications

### 3. KCN Nano Banana World Design
Virtual architecture + design + construction platform. 3D house massing, AI Design Swarm (Architecture / Interior / Landscape / Builder teams), materials, costing, job tracking.

### 4. KCN Human AI Personal Growth & Development
Personal Discovery Profile, AI Career & Skills Map, Proof-of-Skill living portfolio, privacy-first companion — matching the original self-discovery vision.
